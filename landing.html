What Is Sarcopenia?

Sarcopenia is the gradual, age-related loss of muscle mass, strength, and function. It begins as early as your 30s and accelerates after 60 — but it is not inevitable.

Left unchecked, it leads to reduced mobility, increased fracture risk, loss of independence, and a significantly lower quality of life.

Warning Signs to Watch For

  • !Difficulty carrying groceries or opening jars
  • !Feeling unsteady on stairs or uneven ground
  • !Needing to use your arms to push up from a chair
  • !Walking more slowly than you used to
  • !One or more falls in the past 12 months

The good news: Science is clear that progressive resistance training — done correctly for your age and fitness level — can rebuild lost muscle at any age. You do not have to accept decline as the default.
